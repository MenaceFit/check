1. End in Europe35 s

Thank you. By 1944, the Allies were ready to attack in the west. On 6 June 1944, D-Day, more than 150,000 soldiers landed in Normandy, from the sea and from the sky. It was the biggest amphibious invasion in history. Paris was freed in August. At the same time, the Soviets advanced from the east. In April 1945 they reached Berlin, Hitler killed himself, and Germany surrendered on 8 May 1945.

2. Holocaust30 s

We also have to talk about the Holocaust. The Nazis wanted to destroy the Jewish people of Europe. They sent millions of people to concentration and extermination camps, like Auschwitz. About six million Jews were murdered. The Nazis also killed Roma people, disabled people and political opponents. It is the darkest part of the war, and we must remember it so that it never happens again.

3. War in Asia35 s

But the war was not over in Asia. Japan refused to surrender. The USA had secretly built the atomic bomb in the Manhattan Project. On 6 August 1945, an American plane dropped a bomb on Hiroshima, and three days later another on Nagasaki. More than 200,000 people died, mostly civilians. Japan surrendered on 15 August, and the war officially ended on 2 September 1945. People still debate whether the bombs were necessary.

4. Consequences30 s

What were the consequences? Between 70 and 85 million people died, and many cities in Europe and Asia were destroyed. In 1945 the United Nations was created to keep peace. Nazi leaders were judged at the Nuremberg trials. Europe was divided between the USA and the USSR, which led to the Cold War. And in Africa and Asia, many colonies began to demand independence.

5. Timeline20 s

Let’s recap with a timeline.

  • 1939–41: the Axis advances almost everywhere.
  • 1942–43: the tide turns: Midway, El Alamein, Stalingrad.
  • 1944–45: the Allies win: D-Day, liberation of Paris, fall of Berlin, end of the war in the Pacific.
All 3 speakers10 s

To conclude, the Second World War was a tragedy that changed the world forever. Thank you for listening. Do you have any questions?
